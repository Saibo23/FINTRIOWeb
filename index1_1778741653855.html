FAQ

Got Questions?
We Have Answers.

How do I track my bus or cab in real-time? ▼
All our vehicles are GPS-enabled. Simply go to "My Bookings" in the app or website, tap on your active booking, and click "Live Track." You can also share the tracking link with family members for safety.
Can I cancel or reschedule my booking? ▼
Yes! You can reschedule or cancel your booking anytime from the app. Cancellations made 4+ hours before departure are eligible for a full refund. Flexi Pass holders enjoy free rescheduling at any time.
What payment methods are accepted? ▼
We accept all major payment methods — UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, net banking, and popular wallets. EMI options are also available for bookings above ₹5,000.
Is TravelMe safe for solo women travelers? ▼
Absolutely. All our drivers are police-verified and trained in safety standards. Our SOS button in the app directly connects to emergency services. Live tracking can be shared with trusted contacts at any time.
Do you offer corporate travel solutions? ▼
Yes! We offer dedicated corporate accounts with centralized billing, employee travel management dashboards, customized routes, and a dedicated account manager. Contact us for a custom quote.
How do I get the best deal on flights? ▼
Set fare alerts on our app for your preferred routes. Booking 3-6 weeks in advance and flying mid-week usually gives the best prices. TravelMe also offers exclusive app-only discounts and cashback offers.
